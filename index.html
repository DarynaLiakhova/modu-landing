    Good to know

    Questions, answered.

    Can't find what you need? Write to us — we answer within one working day.

    Do I need any experience?

    No. Every piece is laser-cut and numbered, and the illustrated manual shows one step at a time. The kits are rated 4 out of 5 for difficulty and recommended from age 14, so expect a build that asks for patience rather than skill.

    How long does a build take?

    Romantic Carousel takes about 4.5 hours, Corner Coffee Shop about 5 hours and Marble Night City about 7 hours. Most people spread a build over two or three evenings.

    Do I need tools, glue or batteries?

    Marble Night City and Romantic Carousel slot together without glue and run on a hand crank and a wind-up spring, so they need no batteries. Corner Coffee Shop needs craft glue for its small details and two AAA batteries for the LED lights. Neither is in the box.

    What if a piece is missing or breaks?

    Write to us with the model name and the number printed next to the piece on its wooden sheet. We will send you a replacement.

    Shipping and returns?

    We ship from Bratislava across the EU, and the shipping cost is shown at checkout. You can return an unopened kit within 14 days of delivery.
