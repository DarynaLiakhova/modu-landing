    Description

    A mechanical night city in miniature. Turn the handle and steel marbles race over multi-level tracks, climb the lift and drop through a layered labyrinth.

    It runs on a hand crank and gravity, with clear acrylic parts that show every loop and junction. Charcoal wood with metallic print gives it an industrial look on a shelf.

    Assembly & care

    Estimated build time: about 7 hours. No glue and no batteries needed.

    Every joint slots together, so check that each one sits tight before you move on. Rated 4 of 5 for difficulty. Keep the finished model dry and out of direct sunlight, and dust it with a soft dry brush.
