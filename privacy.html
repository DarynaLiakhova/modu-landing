    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 12 October 2026

    Who we are

    MODU is a brand of BROBAS PARTNERS, 7501 Lemont Rd, Suite 300, Woodridge, IL 60517, USA. BROBAS PARTNERS is responsible for the personal data collected on this website.

    Questions about your data: sales@brobaspartners.com or +1 (872) 267-9878.

    What we collect and why

    Your email address and the kits in your cart. MODU is not open yet. When you press Pay now and leave your email, we save it together with the kits you chose, their total and the date. We use it for one purpose: to tell you when the shop opens. Nothing is charged and no order is placed. The legal basis is your consent, which you give by submitting the form.

    Your cart. The kits you add are kept in your own browser (local storage) so the cart works between pages. This data never leaves your device.

    Visit statistics. If you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics to count visits and clicks, such as how many people press Pay now. Nothing is collected before you accept, and you can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of the page.

    Who processes your data

    Email sign-ups are stored with Formspree, an online form service based in the United States. If analytics is on, statistics are processed by Google. The website is hosted by a third-party hosting provider, and its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. Some of these providers are located outside the European Union.

    How long we keep it

    We keep your email until we send the launch announcement, and no longer than 12 months. You can ask us to delete it earlier at any time.

    Your rights

    You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, withdraw your consent, or object to its use by writing to sales@brobaspartners.com. If you live in the EU, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.
